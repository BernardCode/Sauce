# Daylight motion system

This is the ranked set of 100 motion details used across Daylight. Rank 1 has the highest product value. Rank 100 is the most optional. Lower-ranked effects are not louder. They are rarer, shorter, or only visible after direct interaction.

Every effect follows four rules:

* Feedback starts immediately. Motion never delays a local update or navigation.
* Most movement lasts 120 to 280 milliseconds. Entrances can last up to 440 milliseconds.
* Continuous motion is limited to status dots, loading, and very slow background ambience.
* `prefers-reduced-motion` disables animation, smooth scrolling, and decorative movement.
